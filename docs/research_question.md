# Research question

> Can an independent team recover the hidden peak of the 98-qubit P12 circuit using a transparent,
> preregistered and reproducible Quantinuum execution and post-processing protocol?

Scientific hypothesis: shots from a suitable current Quantinuum system contain sufficient aggregate
signal for bitwise majority to recover a high percentage of hidden bits.

Engineering feasibility question: can the original source be frozen, compiled, validated, measured,
and normalized without an untracked qubit/bit permutation?

Primary endpoint: percentage of the 98 hidden bits recovered correctly. Secondary endpoints are
exact recovery, Hamming distance, bootstrap stability, per-bit confidence, method agreement, runtime,
and compilation overhead.

Nonclaims: Milestone 1 does not recover P12, establish hardware executability, demonstrate quantum
advantage, or establish that small-circuit validation proves full-circuit semantics.
